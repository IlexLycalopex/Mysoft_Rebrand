Purpose of this document

This is the master source document for Mysoft's AI Transformation Journey client output framework. Each stage section covers: readiness conditions, Mysoft recommendations by category, client obligations by timing, system configuration requirements (Intacct, X3, X3CloudDocs), success metrics, commercial outputs, and expansion triggers.

This document is internal. Client-facing outputs are derived from this source and edited for audience and context before delivery.

  Mysoft Recommendations and Actions — Stage 1

Discovery and Scoping

Conduct a structured Finance Operations Review covering: current system architecture, data flow, close cycle timeline, approval matrix, reconciliation processes, and known pain points. This becomes the baseline document for the entire engagement.

Map all finance workflows to identify which are candidates for Stage 2 automation — document this now, even though automation is not the current scope. This protects the commercial pathway and avoids having to re-discover in six months.

Identify shadow systems (Excel models, third-party point solutions, manual journals) and agree a migration or retirement plan before go-live.

Run the AI Readiness Assessment at discovery stage to establish the Domain A and Domain D baseline scores. This creates the forward roadmap narrative from the first client meeting.

Agree the Process Governance Map scope with the client CFO. Even at Stage 1, document what will be automated and what will remain manual — this becomes Layer 1 of the governance framework.

Implementation — Sage Intacct

Configure the dimensional chart of accounts to support multi-entity, multi-location, and project-level reporting. Dimensions set at Stage 1 cannot easily be restructured later — get this right first time.

Implement Intacct approval workflows for purchase orders, invoices, and expense claims from day one. Even at Stage 1, system-enforced approvals are non-negotiable; they are the foundation of the Layer 2 governance model.

Configure Intacct's native audit trail and user permission model to align with the client's existing approval authority matrix. Document the mapping as a named Mysoft deliverable.

Set up Intacct dashboards and reporting for CFO and Finance Director from go-live: real-time cash position, AP ageing, AR ageing, and period-close status. These replace manual reporting immediately and demonstrate value on day one.

Implement bank reconciliation through Intacct. Automated bank feeds and reconciliation are the first automation win at Stage 1 and build confidence in the platform's data integrity.

Configure multi-entity consolidation if applicable. The consolidation architecture set at Stage 1 directly enables AI-assisted group reporting at Stage 3.

Establish the close cycle calendar within Intacct, with period-end checklists and sign-off workflows. A structured, system-governed close process is the prerequisite for AI-assisted close at Stage 3.

Implementation — Sage X3

Configure the core operational modules required for the client's sector: manufacturing (production orders, BOM, routings), distribution (purchase orders, sales orders, stock management), or food and beverage (catch weight, traceability, quality management). Scope is client-specific but must be complete at Stage 1.

Set up X3 financial reporting structure: legal entities, sites, analytical dimensions. The analytical dimension design at Stage 1 determines the depth of operational insight available at Stage 2 and above.

Configure X3 approval workflows for procurement and finance sign-off. Approval rules set now become the threshold framework for X3CloudDocs automation at Stage 2.

Implement X3 supplier and customer master data governance. Clean master data is the foundation for automated invoice matching and order processing at Stage 2. Establish data quality rules and ownership before go-live.

Establish X3 period-close procedures and lock controls. Financial period lock configuration in X3 is the control environment foundation — set it correctly and document the process.

Configure X3 intercompany and multi-site transactions if applicable. The intercompany design at Stage 1 directly affects the scope of automation available at Stage 2.

Data Migration and Quality

Define and execute a data quality baseline before migration: supplier master, customer master, chart of accounts, open transactions. Do not migrate poor data — use the implementation as the forcing function to clean it.

Conduct a structured data migration validation against the pre-agreed acceptance criteria. Deliver a signed data migration sign-off document before go-live. This protects Mysoft and gives the client CFO an auditable record.

Document all data transformation rules applied during migration. This is required if the client is subject to financial regulation or audit and should be standard regardless.

Establish ongoing data governance: who owns supplier master, customer master, and chart of accounts maintenance. AI quality at Stage 3 is directly dependent on data governance established at Stage 1.

Training and Adoption

Deliver role-specific training, not system-feature training. Finance Manager training should cover: close process, approval workflows, exception handling, and reporting. CFO/FD training should cover: dashboards, variance analysis, and the audit trail. Do not deliver generic ERP training.

Conduct a structured go-live readiness review with the client's Finance Director before cutover. Confirm: all open items resolved, approval workflows tested, user access confirmed, first-period close plan agreed.

Deliver a 30-day post-go-live support plan with named Mysoft contact and defined escalation path. Early adoption issues that are not resolved quickly erode confidence and damage the expansion pathway.

Governance and Documentation

Deliver the Governance Configuration Record as a standard go-live document: approval thresholds, user permissions, workflow rules, period-lock procedures, and data ownership. Version-controlled and client-signed.

Draft the Process Governance Map for Stage 2 (even if Stage 2 is not in scope). Identifying the automation candidates now positions the QAOR conversation and creates forward commercial momentum.

Begin the AI Readiness Assessment scoring update post-go-live: Domain A should now be at 2-3. Record this as evidence of Mysoft-delivered readiness progression.

  Mysoft Recommendations and Actions — Stage 2

X3CloudDocs — Purchase Invoice Automation (PIA)

Run the PIA scoping workshop: map the current invoice receipt and processing workflow, document all invoice types (PO-matched, non-PO, credit notes, recurring), identify the exception categories and their current handling, and confirm the volume and value distribution across supplier tiers.

Configure PIA for automated three-way matching: purchase order, goods receipt, and supplier invoice. Set matching tolerances at the level agreed in the Process Governance Map — typically ±2-5% for value and exact match for quantity, client-specific.

Configure PIA exception routing: invoices that fail matching go to the named Finance Manager (Tier 2 review) within the 48-hour SLA. Invoices above the Tier 3 threshold go directly to Finance Director. Do not allow exceptions to queue without an SLA.

Set up PIA for e-invoicing compliance where the client has European supplier relationships or anticipates UK e-invoicing mandate requirements. Configure for Peppol network receipt where applicable.

Configure PIA supplier portal (if in scope) for supplier self-service invoice submission and status tracking. Reduces inbound supplier queries and AP team administration.

Deliver PIA go-live with a parallel run period of a minimum of two weeks: process invoices through both old and new routes, reconcile daily, confirm match rates before full cutover.

Establish PIA KPI reporting from day one: match rate (target 85%+ at launch, 90%+ by month 3), exception rate, processing time per invoice, cycle time from receipt to payment approval.

X3CloudDocs — Sales Order Automation (SOA)

Run the SOA scoping workshop: map all inbound order channels (EDI, email, web, phone, customer portal), document order types, identify exception triggers (credit hold, out-of-stock, special pricing), and confirm volume and channel distribution.

Configure SOA for automated order ingestion from EDI and email channels. Set up order validation rules aligned to the client's credit and pricing policy — orders that do not pass validation route to the order management team (Tier 2).

Configure SOA exception handling: credit hold escalation, out-of-stock notification, and special pricing approval routing, all within the Tiered Approval Model.

Set up SOA EDI mapping for top-ten customer trading partners. EDI configuration requires customer-specific mapping — prioritise by volume. Remaining customers can be onboarded in waves post-launch.

Configure SOA confirmation and despatch notification workflows through X3CloudDocs ENA (if in scope) — automated order acknowledgement, despatch notification, and proof of delivery documentation reduce inbound customer queries.

Deliver SOA go-live with parallel processing period. Confirm order capture accuracy and exception rates before full channel cutover.

Establish SOA KPI reporting: orders processed without manual intervention (target 75%+ at launch), order-to-despatch cycle time, exception rate by order type, EDI partner uptime.

X3CloudDocs — Electronic Document Management (EDM)

Configure EDM as the document repository for all X3 financial documents: purchase invoices, sales invoices, delivery notes, contracts, and correspondence. EDM is the audit trail infrastructure for Stage 3 AI governance.

Set up EDM retention policies aligned to statutory requirements: seven years minimum for financial documents, longer for contracts. Configure automatic archival and access controls.

Configure EDM document search and retrieval for Finance Director and external auditor use. Auditors should be able to retrieve any document by transaction reference, date range, or document type within seconds.

Integrate EDM with X3 transaction records so that every transaction has its source documents attached and retrievable. This is the configuration that makes the AI audit trail possible at Stage 3.

Configure EDM for HMRC Making Tax Digital compliance where applicable: VAT return supporting documentation, digital record keeping requirements.

X3CloudDocs — Email and Notification Automation (ENA)

Configure ENA for AP payment notification: automated remittance advice to suppliers on payment run, reducing inbound supplier queries and AP team time.

Configure ENA for AR dunning automation: automated payment reminder sequences by customer segment and ageing bracket. Approval authority rules apply — ENA does not send to customers flagged for escalation without Finance Manager review.

Configure ENA for internal approval notifications: Tier 2 and Tier 3 approval requests delivered by ENA with direct action links. Remove email-based approval — all approvals through the system.

Configure ENA for exception alerts: Finance Director and CFO receive exception summary notification at agreed frequency (daily or weekly). This is the Layer 2 operational reporting output.

Set ENA volume thresholds and suppression rules to avoid notification fatigue. If a recipient is receiving more than a defined number of notifications per day, escalate to Mysoft for workflow review.

Sage Intacct Automation

Activate Intacct AP automation: automated invoice capture (OCR or integrated submission), three-way matching against POs and receipts, and routing to approval workflow. Configure matching tolerances and exception rules in the Process Governance Map.

Activate Intacct AR automation: automated cash application, payment matching against open invoices, and AR ageing exception flagging. Set up automated dunning email sequences through Intacct communications.

Configure Intacct Intelligent GL for anomaly detection: activate GL anomaly alerts for unusual posting patterns, duplicate payments, and threshold breaches. This is the first AI-adjacent capability in Intacct and should be demonstrated to the CFO as a Stage 3 preview.

Configure Intacct vendor payment automation: electronic payment file generation (BACS, SEPA, ACH for US entities), payment approval workflow, and payment confirmation receipt.

Set up Intacct contract and revenue recognition automation where applicable (SaaS, professional services, subscription businesses). Revenue recognition rules configured at Stage 2 are the foundation for AI-assisted revenue forecasting at Stage 3.

Configure Intacct project accounting if applicable: time and expense capture, project billing automation, project profitability reporting. For professional services clients, this is often the primary Stage 2 value driver.

Activate Intacct Spend Management for employee expense automation: mobile receipt capture, automated policy checking, approval routing, and GL posting. Removes a common remaining manual process after AP automation.

Governance — Layer 1 and Layer 2 Activation

Finalise and client-sign the Process Governance Map: confirm automation tier for each workflow, document matching tolerances, exception triggers, and approval routing rules. This is a named Mysoft deliverable and the first formal governance document.

Configure and document the Tiered Approval Model in the system: Tier 1 automation rules, Tier 2 notification SLAs, Tier 3 mandatory review triggers, Tier 4 CFO escalation conditions. Each tier must be testable and auditable.

Establish the first Quarterly AI Optimisation Review at go-live: schedule it for 90 days post-launch. The first QAOR covers: match rates vs target, exception rate calibration, threshold review, and Stage 3 readiness assessment.

Deliver the Layer 1 and Layer 2 Governance Configuration Record update: add Stage 2 automation rules to the Stage 1 record. This is now the current governance baseline.

Configure the AI action log for all automated actions: every Tier 1 action, Tier 2 notification, and override decision is logged with timestamp, user, and outcome. This is the audit trail that Layer 3 builds on.

Intacct AI Capabilities — Activation and Configuration

Conduct the Stage 3 AI capability activation workshop with the CFO and Finance Manager: walk through each live Intacct AI feature, demonstrate the output, agree the activation sequence, and add each to the Process Governance Map with its automation tier classification.

Activate Intacct Intelligent GL for full anomaly detection: configure detection sensitivity, define the alert threshold that triggers Tier 2 review, and confirm that anomaly alerts are routed to the correct approver. Demonstrate the first alert cycle to the Finance Director before full activation.

Activate AI-assisted forecasting: configure the forecasting model inputs (revenue drivers, cost structure, working capital cycles), agree the forecasting horizon (rolling 12-month recommended), and confirm the approval workflow for forecast publication to the board. Forecasts are AI-generated but CFO-approved before board use.

Activate variance commentary generation for the management accounts close: configure the narrative parameters (material variance threshold, commentary format, sector and business context), and run the first AI-generated commentary pack alongside the manual version for CFO comparison before full adoption.

Activate natural language reporting for board pack preparation: configure the report templates, agree the data scope, and train Finance Director and CFO on the query interface. Natural language reporting removes BI dependency for standard board pack production.

Configure AI-assisted cash flow forecasting: integrate AR ageing, AP payment schedule, payroll, and known commitments into the Intacct cash flow model. Confirm the Tier 3 review requirement for cash flow forecasts used in banking or investor reporting.

  Mysoft Recommendations and Actions — Stage 4

Agentic Workflow Design

Conduct the Agentic Finance Design Workshop with CFO, Finance Director, and board sponsor. Map the target state: which workflows will be fully agentic, which will remain human-in-the-loop, and which will remain fully human. This is a strategic, not technical, conversation — outputs are a workflow map and a governance brief, not a software specification.

Define the agentic scope precisely in the Process Governance Map update: each agentic workflow must have a named human owner, defined exception criteria, documented override protocol, and explicit statement of what the agent cannot do. Scope creep in agentic deployment is a governance failure.

Design the human-and-AI workforce architecture: which roles change, which new roles are created (exception manager, AI governance reviewer, AI optimisation analyst), and how performance management adapts. This is a People and Culture deliverable, not just a technology deliverable. Mysoft should involve Kim Rix or the client equivalent.

Sequence the agentic workflow deployment in waves: Wave 1 — highest-volume, lowest-risk workflows (payment run preparation, recurring invoice processing, routine bank reconciliation). Wave 2 — higher-value workflows with stronger governance (multi-entity consolidation, AI-assisted audit pack preparation). Wave 3 — complex or novel workflows (agentic cash flow management, AI-directed working capital optimisation). Each wave requires QAOR sign-off before the next begins.

Define the agentic failure protocol: what happens when an agentic workflow encounters a scenario outside its defined parameters. The failure protocol must be documented, tested, and communicated to all stakeholders before Wave 1 goes live.

Establish the AI model performance review within the QAOR: at Stage 4, the QAOR includes a structured assessment of agentic workflow performance — action accuracy rate, exception rate, override rate, and any unintended outputs. This review is the primary governance evidence for auditors.

Intacct Platform and Configuration

Validate the client's ERP platform agentic workflow capability against the design brief. For Sage Intacct: confirm which workflows are deliverable within the current release, which require Sage roadmap commitment, and which require third-party AI orchestration. For Sage X3: confirm X3 agentic integration points, X3CloudDocs automation layer coverage, and any third-party orchestration tools required to achieve the agreed agentic scope.

Configure agentic approval workflows within Intacct: payment run agents, reconciliation agents, and reporting agents each require separate workflow configuration with explicit Tier 1-4 classification. No agentic workflow operates above Tier 1 without a human review gate.

Configure the Stage 4 AI action log: all agentic actions must generate a log entry with: action taken, confidence level, exception trigger (if any), and any human review outcome. The log must be structured for auditor export.

Integrate agentic workflows with the broader finance technology stack: ERP, treasury management, payroll, and CRM. Document all integration points and data flows in the Governance Configuration Record.

Establish agentic workflow monitoring: real-time dashboards for Finance Director and CFO showing agentic action volume, exception rate, SLA performance, and anomaly flags. Exception rates above threshold trigger automatic QAOR escalation.

Configure model drift detection: establish baseline performance metrics for each agentic workflow and alert thresholds for performance degradation. A workflow whose accuracy falls below threshold is automatically suspended pending QAOR review.

Board and Regulatory Governance

Update the Board AI Governance Report for Stage 4: include agentic workflow scope, human oversight architecture, exception rates, audit trail evidence, and the QAOR cycle record. This report is the primary board governance document and should be presented at the first board meeting of each financial year.

Develop the Regulatory and Audit Engagement Plan: brief external auditors on agentic workflow governance before the first audit cycle where agentic workflows are material. Prepare structured responses to anticipated auditor questions (see governance framework Section 6).

Conduct an annual Agentic Finance Governance Review: broader than the quarterly QAOR, this review covers: scope expansion assessment, regulatory landscape changes, Intacct AI roadmap alignment, and the governance model adequacy for the next 12 months.

Engage with the client's insurers or risk advisors on the agentic workflow governance model. Directors and Officers insurance and professional indemnity coverage may require notification of material AI deployment in finance.

Publish the client's AI governance posture internally: all finance team members should understand what is automated, what escalates to them, and what remains human. Internal communication of the governance model reduces risk of unofficial AI tool adoption outside the framework.

People and Organisation

Redesign the Finance Manager role specification to reflect Stage 4 responsibilities: exception oversight, AI governance reviewer, process optimisation analyst. The role evolves from transaction processor to AI supervisor — this requires formal role redesign, not just informal expectation change.

Establish the AI Governance Reviewer role (or assign the function within an existing role): responsible for monthly AI action log review, exception rate monitoring, and QAOR preparation. This is a permanent operational role, not a project role.

Develop finance team capability in AI governance: training on understanding AI output confidence indicators, exception trigger review, override decision-making, and escalation protocol. This is not technology training — it is governance judgement training.

Review the finance function headcount model: Stage 4 should demonstrate that revenue growth no longer requires proportional finance headcount growth. Model the capacity released by agentic deployment and agree with the CFO how that capacity is redeployed (commercial finance, FP&A, strategic advisory) rather than reduced.
