1. Strategic Intent

Mysoft’s go-to-market is built around a single, defensible position: we are the partner that helps ambitious organisations build finance and operations functions ready for growth — not just today, but for the business they are becoming.

This is an AI-led digital transformation story. Not an efficiency argument. Not a cost-reduction narrative. A growth story, underpinned by the operational infrastructure, automation foundations, and AI capabilities that allow finance and operations to scale with the business rather than constrain it.

This framework applies equally to Sage X3 and Sage Intacct engagements. The maturity journey, governance model, buying triggers, and commercial architecture are consistent across both platforms. Where capabilities differ, this document notes the platform-specific approach. The partner ISV ecosystem extends the value of both platforms across the full operational footprint.

2. Market Context

Four converging forces are reshaping the mid-market finance and operations function and creating the buying environment Mysoft is positioned to lead.

2.1 AI Is Moving from Experimentation to Deployment

CFOs are no longer asking whether AI is relevant to finance. They are being asked by their boards to articulate a credible AI strategy. Most cannot yet, because their operational foundations — process maturity, data quality, and ERP infrastructure — are not ready to support it. That creates a major consultative opportunity for a partner who can design the journey, not just supply a platform.

2.2 Finance and Operations Talent Constraints Are Structural, Not Cyclical

Hiring experienced finance and operational professionals in the UK mid-market is increasingly difficult and expensive. The response cannot be to keep adding headcount to compensate for process gaps. Organisations that build the right infrastructure now gain a structural advantage: the ability to grow revenue without proportional growth in operating cost.

2.3 ERP Is Becoming a System of Intelligence

Cloud ERP is no longer a differentiator. What differentiates modern platforms is their capacity to act as an AI orchestration layer: connecting data, automating workflows, and surfacing insight at speed. Sage X3 and Sage Intacct are positioned to deliver this — but only when implemented and configured by a partner who understands the operating model, not just the software. The partner ISV ecosystem extends this intelligence across warehousing, manufacturing, logistics, and people.

2.4 The Competitive Whitespace

Most Sage partners are still leading with cloud, dashboards, and efficiency. Very few can credibly articulate:

AI operating model design across finance and operations

Agentic finance workflow governance

Human-and-AI workforce architecture

Incremental AI maturity journeys with commercial milestones

An integrated partner ISV ecosystem that extends transformation beyond the core ERP

That whitespace is Mysoft’s to occupy.

5. Buying Trigger Framework

The GTM is structured around four recognisable buying triggers. These are the moments when a CFO or Operations Director will engage with content, take a call, or open a conversation with a partner.

Growth Acceleration

Revenue growing faster than the back office can support. Month-end is slipping. Consolidations are manual. Reporting is backward-looking.
Entry: “When your finance and operations function becomes a growth bottleneck.”

Scale Ambition

Board or investor expectation of significant revenue growth. The CFO and COO know current systems and operating model will not support the business at 2x or 3x current size.
Entry: “Finance and operations infrastructure designed for the business you are building.”

AI Mandate

Board or CEO has asked for a credible AI strategy. The CFO has no structured answer yet and recognises that operational foundations need addressing before AI can be deployed safely.
Entry: “What leaders need to do before deploying AI in finance and operations.”

Compliance or Operational Event

A near-miss, acquisition, new reporting obligation, supply chain disruption, or regulatory change that has exposed process fragility.
Entry: “What robust controls and operational resilience actually look like in a scaling business.”

10. Pre-Execution Requirements

Two strategic questions must be resolved before this GTM goes to market:

10.1 AI Delivery Credibility

The messaging positions Mysoft as an AI-enabled transformation partner. The evidence base for that claim must be defined before it goes to market. This means documenting:

Delivered client outcomes involving automation or AI-adjacent capability — on both X3 and Intacct

Internal tooling and AI capability within the Mysoft consulting team

Trained consultants who can design AI readiness assessments credibly

Proprietary frameworks: AI Readiness Assessment, Agentic Finance Governance Framework, AI Transformation Master Framework

10.2 Platform AI Capability Mapping

The AI maturity ladder requires a current, demonstrable mapping of what AI features are live and available today on each platform. Sales must be able to demo Stages 2 and 3 concretely rather than conceptually. This requires:

A current Sage Intacct AI feature inventory (Copilot, Intelligent GL, forecasting) aligned to maturity stages

A current Sage X3 AI and analytics capability map: X3 native analytics, SEI, Phocas, ZAP BI integration points

X3CloudDocs capability map: PIA, SOA, EDM, ENA — demonstration scripts and readiness thresholds

Demonstration scripts for AI-assisted reporting, Sage Copilot (Intacct), and ISV BI tools (X3)

Clear guidance on what is available now versus what is on the Sage and partner roadmaps

Pre-Execution Gate

Resolve both points before activating Phase 2 content and any event that positions Mysoft as an AI transformation partner. The claim must be substantiated before it is made publicly.
