About Mysoft

Careers at Mysoft.

We are building the leading AI-enabled finance transformation practice in the UK Sage ecosystem. If you want to work at the intersection of ERP, AI, and strategic finance advisory — with customers who are making real transformation decisions — we want to hear from you.

Why Mysoft

What makes Mysoft a different place to work.

You work on complex, interesting problems

Our customers are mid-market businesses making multi-year technology decisions with real strategic consequences. The work is not trivial — it requires judgement, domain expertise, and the ability to challenge assumptions constructively.

You work with proprietary IP

X3CloudDocs is Mysoft-owned. Working here means contributing to a product that has no equivalent in the Sage ecosystem — and developing the skills that make that possible. That is a different career development path from working at a pure reseller.

Senior colleagues, not junior competition

We are deliberate about maintaining a senior consultant ratio. Junior staff are supported by experienced practitioners, not deployed independently on critical paths. Career progression is real and documented — not promised.

UK and North America

International engagement capability means exposure to a broader range of customers and contexts than a UK-only practice. For consultants interested in international work, opportunities exist within the current portfolio.
