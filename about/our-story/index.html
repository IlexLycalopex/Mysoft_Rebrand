Who We Are

A trusted advisor. An innovator. A customer-centric partner.

Mysoft was founded on the conviction that mid-market businesses deserve the same quality of ERP expertise and strategic advisory as enterprise organisations — without the enterprise overhead. We were among the first Sage X3 partners in the UK, and over more than two decades that founding conviction has shaped how we work, the depth of expertise we have built, and the proprietary IP we have created.

Today, we are the UK's leading Sage partner for Sage X3. We are also a major Sage Intacct partner — operating across both the UK and North America. That dual-geography, dual-platform capability is uncommon in the Sage partner ecosystem. Most partners operate in one geography and one product line. We operate in two of each — with the implementation track record and the Sage Platinum status to evidence it.

X3CloudDocs — our proprietary document automation and management product — is the top-rated product on the Sage X3 Marketplace and the clearest evidence of our commitment to building capabilities that no other partner has. It is not licensed-in technology. It is Mysoft-developed, Mysoft-owned IP that delivers competitive advantage for every Sage X3 customer we work with.

Our current positioning is unambiguous: we are an AI-enabled finance and operations transformation partner. Not a software reseller. Not a VAR with a technology dependency. A transformation partner with the IP, the methodology, and the delivery capability to move finance and operations functions from where they are to where they need to be.
