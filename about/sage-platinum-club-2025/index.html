About Mysoft

Sage Platinum Club 2025.

Mysoft achieved Sage Platinum Partner status in 2025 — the highest tier in the Sage partner ecosystem, awarded on implementation quality, customer satisfaction, and technical depth. Not a marketing designation; a structured quality assessment by Sage.

What Platinum Means

The criteria behind the status.

Sage Platinum Partner status is awarded annually to the highest-performing partners in the Sage ecosystem. It is not awarded on sales volume alone — the assessment includes customer satisfaction scores, implementation quality metrics, technical certification levels, and partner retention rates.

For organisations evaluating ERP partners, Platinum status provides independent evidence of delivery quality that no partner-produced marketing material can replicate. The assessors are Sage, not the partner.

Customer satisfaction

Sage measures customer satisfaction directly with partner customers — independently of the partner. Our scores reflect the quality of delivery and the strength of the long-term relationships we build with the organisations we work with.

Implementation quality

Project delivery against scope, timeline, and budget are tracked by Sage for Platinum assessment. Our delivery track record — maintained consistently across both Sage X3 and Sage Intacct implementations — underpins the status.

Technical certification

Platinum status requires a minimum number of Sage-certified consultants at the required certification levels — across all relevant product lines. Our entire delivery team holds current Sage certification.

What It Means for You

Why partner tier matters when you are choosing an ERP partner.

A well-implemented mid-tier ERP platform consistently outperforms a poorly implemented tier-one platform. The same principle applies to partner quality: the implementation partner's capability determines outcomes more than the platform's feature set in the majority of cases.

Platinum status is the most reliable independent signal of partner quality available to organisations evaluating Sage partners. It is not sufficient on its own — reference conversations and demonstrated sector experience matter too — but it is the right starting filter.
