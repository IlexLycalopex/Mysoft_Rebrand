ERP Selection Roundtable — May 2026

Mysoft hosted an ERP selection roundtable in May 2026 for CFOs and Finance Directors at active buying stage. The session covered platform selection criteria, evaluation process design, vendor risk assessment, and the questions most RFP processes miss.

Twelve finance leaders attended, representing manufacturing, professional services, and NFP organisations. Post-event feedback indicated that the structured evaluation framework was the most valuable output — several participants had not previously defined their selection criteria with sufficient rigour to differentiate between shortlisted platforms.
