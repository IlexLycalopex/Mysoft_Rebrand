Sector Requirements

What Financial Services Finance Functions Require

Regulatory Compliance and Audit Readiness

FCA-regulated businesses require financial systems with demonstrable controls — segregation of duties, approval workflow documentation, and field-level audit trails. Sage Intacct's SOC 1 and SOC 2 certifications and comprehensive audit trail architecture are designed for exactly this regulatory context.

Multi-Entity and Fund Structure

Asset managers, PE firms, and holding companies with multiple funds, SPVs, or operating entities need native multi-entity consolidation — with intercompany transactions, management fee allocation, and fund-level reporting handled automatically rather than through manual adjustment.

Revenue Recognition for Fee Income

Management fees, performance fees, and advisory fees each have distinct recognition patterns. Sage Intacct's revenue recognition module handles the full complexity of financial services fee income — including deferred fee recognition and clawback provisions — natively.

Investor and LP Reporting

Investor reporting in financial services requires financial data that is accurate to the day, formatted to institutional standards, and backed by complete audit trail. Sage Intacct's reporting infrastructure supports this without manual assembly or version-control risk.
