Sector Requirements

What Food and Beverage ERP Must Handle

Batch Traceability

End-to-end lot traceability from raw material receipt through production to customer despatch. Recall readiness requires the ability to identify every affected batch and every customer that received product within minutes — not days.

Shelf-Life and Date Management

FEFO (First Expired, First Out) picking, best-before date management, and automated near-expiry alerts integrated with procurement and production planning. Reduces waste and prevents recall exposure.

Recipe and Formulation Costing

Variable recipe costing, yield management, and co-product and by-product accounting. Accurate product cost calculations that update automatically when ingredient costs change — not quarterly manual recalculations.

Regulatory and Certification Compliance

BRC, SALSA, and retailer audit documentation. Allergen management, label compliance, and specification control integrated with production — so compliance documentation is a system output, not a manual exercise.

Promotional Pricing and Customer Management

Complex customer price agreements, promotional pricing, and retrospective discount management — typical in food retail supply — handled natively within the sales order management module.

EDI and Retailer Integration

Purchase order, despatch advice, and invoice EDI with major UK and European retailers. X3CloudDocs handles electronic notification and acknowledgement within the Sage X3 environment.
