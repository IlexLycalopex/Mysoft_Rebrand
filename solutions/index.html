Platform Orientation

Finance-first vs. operations-first.

Sage Intacct is a cloud-native financial management platform — built for organisations where the finance function is the primary system of record. It excels in multi-entity consolidation, dimensional reporting, project accounting, and revenue recognition. It is the right platform when the business complexity lives in the finance layer.

Sage X3 is a full ERP platform — built for organisations where finance, supply chain, production, and purchasing must be managed as a single integrated system. When operational complexity drives reporting complexity, X3 is typically the better fit. X3CloudDocs, Mysoft's proprietary automation product, extends X3 with the document intelligence that finance teams need.

Most organisations do not need to choose between platforms on feature lists. They need to identify which architecture fits their business model — and then ensure the implementation partner has sector depth to configure it correctly.

Financial management

Core accounting, reporting, and close cycle capabilities

● Best-in-class
  • Dimensional accounting — unlimited custom dimensions
  • Automated close and period management
  • Sage Copilot AI for variance commentary and forecasting
  • Native revenue recognition (ASC 606 / IFRS 15)
  • Real-time dashboards — no month-end reporting lag
○ Capable
  • Strong multi-currency and multi-legislation accounting
  • Integrated cost accounting tied to operations
  • Sage Copilot AI integration available
  • Financial reporting via Velixo or native Crystal Reports
  • Best where operational costing drives finance, not vice versa
Multi-entity & consolidation

Group structures, intercompany, and consolidated reporting

● Best-in-class
  • Native multi-entity — no additional licences per entity
  • Automated intercompany eliminations
  • Real-time consolidated reporting across all entities
  • Multi-currency translation with audit trail
  • Designed for groups growing by acquisition
○ Capable
  • Multi-site and multi-legislation built in
  • Intercompany transactions within X3 sites
  • Consolidated reporting requires additional tooling or BI
  • Stronger where operational sites share supply chain than where legal entities require standalone accounts
Operations & supply chain

Inventory, procurement, production, and fulfilment

— Not applicable
  • Intacct is a finance platform — it does not manage inventory, production, or supply chain natively
  • Integrated with best-of-breed operational tools via API
  • Right when the business runs operationally on a separate system and needs a finance layer on top
● Best-in-class
  • Full MRP and production planning
  • Procurement and purchase order management
  • Warehouse management and serial/lot tracking
  • FEFO, batch management, and traceability
  • Sales order through delivery — fully integrated with finance
