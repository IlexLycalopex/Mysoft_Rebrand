Sector Challenges

What Makes Manufacturing and Distribution Finance Complex

Inventory Valuation Accuracy

FIFO, AVCO, and standard cost methods must remain consistent across sites, currencies, and the supply chain. Organisations running these calculations in spreadsheets or disconnected systems accumulate valuation errors that compound into material misstatements.

Landed Cost and Import Complexity

Freight, duties, insurance, and handling costs need to be allocated to inventory at the line level — not estimated at period end. Without proper landed cost integration, margin reporting is structurally inaccurate.

Production Costing

Bill of materials, work-in-progress valuation, overhead absorption, and variance analysis require a financial system that understands manufacturing processes — not just ledger entries. Cost of goods sold accuracy is a board-level concern.

Multi-Site Coordination

Intersite transfers, shared services allocation, and multi-entity financial consolidation across manufacturing sites create a reconciliation burden that grows with each new location. Organisations managing this manually reach a complexity ceiling well before their growth ceiling.
