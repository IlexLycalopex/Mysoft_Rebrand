NFP and Healthcare Finance

The Compliance and Reporting Requirements That Matter

Fund Accounting and Restricted Funds

Separate tracking of restricted, unrestricted, and temporarily restricted funds — with expenditure tagged to the correct fund at the point of transaction, not allocated after the fact. Sage Intacct's dimensional accounting model makes this automatic rather than manual.

Grant Management and Reporting

Grant lifecycle management — from award through expenditure tracking to final report — with the ability to produce grant-specific financial reports at any point in the grant period. Donor and funder reporting produced from the system, not assembled from spreadsheets.

Statutory and Regulatory Reporting

Charity Commission reporting (UK), SORP compliance, and healthcare regulatory requirements — with the financial data structured to produce required reports directly rather than through manual manipulation. Reduces audit preparation time and auditor query volume.

Programme-Level Financial Visibility

Finance directors in NFP organisations need to see income and expenditure by programme, service, and location — not just by account code. Sage Intacct's multi-dimensional reporting delivers this without a data warehouse or bespoke reporting solution.
