Four Modules

The Complete Automation Suite for Sage X3

PIA

Purchase Invoice Automation

AI-driven invoice capture using OCR field extraction, followed by automated three-way match against the purchase order and goods receipt note. Exceptions are routed to the appropriate tier of the Tiered Approval Model rather than queuing in an inbox.

AI-powered OCR capture — supplier invoices in any format
Automated three-way match (invoice, PO, GRN)
Tiered exception routing (Tier 1–4)
Complete audit trail in Sage X3
Volume threshold for ROI realisation: 300+ invoices per month.

Invoice captured → matched → posted or escalated

SOA

Sales Order Automation

Automated processing of inbound sales orders from customer documents — emails, PDFs, EDI transmissions, or portal uploads. Eliminates manual order keying and reduces order processing cycle time significantly. Each automated order is logged against the relevant Sage X3 transaction.

Multi-channel order intake (email, PDF, EDI, portal)
Intelligent field extraction and customer matching
Direct posting to Sage X3 sales order module
Exception routing for orders that cannot be matched automatically
Volume threshold for ROI realisation: 200+ orders per month.

Order received → extracted → posted to Sage X3

EDM

Electronic Document Management

Governed document archive attached directly to Sage X3 transactions. Every invoice, delivery note, contract, and correspondence is stored, indexed, and retrievable — audit-ready, GDPR-compliant, and accessible from within the ERP.

Document attached to Sage X3 transaction at point of processing
Full-text search across archived documents
GDPR-compliant retention and deletion policies
Auditor access during audit periods without ERP user licences
Volume threshold for ROI realisation: 500+ documents per month.

Document archived → indexed → retrievable in-ERP

ENA

Email and Notification Automation

Intelligent email routing and automated notification workflows triggered by Sage X3 events. Supplier remittances dispatched automatically. Customer statements sent on schedule. Approval request notifications routed to the correct authority. Internal escalations triggered by exception thresholds.

Event-triggered customer and supplier notifications
Approval request routing to tiered authority holders
Automated statement and remittance dispatch
Exception escalation notifications with context

Event triggered → routed → notified automatically

Every X3CloudDocs automated action operates within the Tiered Approval Model. Tiers 1–4 determine which transactions are fully automated, which require notification, and which require direct human review or CFO sign-off.

Every automated action generates an audit trail entry in Sage X3. Timestamped, immutable, and accessible to auditors without requiring ERP user licences.
