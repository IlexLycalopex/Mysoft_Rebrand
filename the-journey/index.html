The AI Transformation Journey

Each stage has defined entry conditions, deliverables, and governance requirements. The AI Readiness Assessment places your organisation at the right starting point.

1 Operational Foundation

Build the Platform Your Business Deserves

Sage X3 or Sage Intacct configured for real-time visibility, governed approvals, and dimensional reporting from day one. The architecture decision that determines whether AI adoption at Stage 3 is straightforward or requires expensive rework.

  • Single source of financial truth across all entities
  • Tiered approval authority built into the system
  • Governance Configuration Record delivered at go-live
  • Stage 2 automation candidates mapped during Stage 1
Explore Stage 1
2 Intelligent Automation

Remove the Manual Transactional Layer. Permanently.

X3CloudDocs for Sage X3 customers. Native Intacct automation for Intacct customers. Tiered Approval Model activated — every automated action has a defined human authority and a complete audit trail.

  • X3CloudDocs: PIA, SOA, EDM, ENA — Mysoft proprietary IP
  • Intacct: Cash Intelligence, AI-powered AP/AR, Intelligent GL
  • Tiered Approval Model configured and activated
  • First QAOR conducted
Explore Stage 2
3 AI-Augmented Finance

Intelligence at the Speed of the Business

Sage Copilot, the Finance Intelligence Agent, Close Workspace, and AI-powered BI. Finance becomes a strategic function. This only works on the governed foundation of Stages 1 and 2.

  • Finance Intelligence Agent — natural language queries
  • Close Workspace and Subledger Reconciliation Assistant
  • BI stack: SEI, Phocas, ZAP BI, Velixo, Swoop Analytics
  • Board AI Governance Report introduced
Explore Stage 3
4 Agentic Finance Operations

Finance at Scale. Without Headcount Growth.

Governed agentic workflows execute defined financial processes autonomously. Every action generates a complete audit trail. Tier 4 authority governs exception escalation. MD sign-off required before Stage 4 is activated.

  • Agentic Design Workshop — mapping autonomous workflow candidates
  • Wave deployment with validation at each activation
  • Full four-layer governance framework active
  • Annual Governance Review on a six-monthly QAOR cycle
