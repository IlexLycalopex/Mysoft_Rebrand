Ecosystem Principles

How the Mysoft ISV ecosystem works

Pre-vetted integrations

Every ISV partner in the Mysoft ecosystem has been assessed for integration quality, support capability, and product roadmap alignment. We do not recommend partners we have not implemented and supported in production environments.

Mapped to maturity stage

ISV partners are mapped to the Mysoft AI Transformation Journey — so the right integrations are introduced at the right stage of maturity. Adding BI tooling before the data foundation is clean is a common and costly mistake; the ecosystem mapping prevents it.

Single integration owner

Mysoft acts as the integration owner for the full ecosystem — co-ordinating between the ERP platform and ISV partners, managing integration testing, and providing first-line support for integration issues. You do not manage three separate vendor relationships.

By Category

Preferred partners across the integration landscape

Business Intelligence & Reporting

Phocas

Purpose-built BI for Sage X3 and Intacct. Pre-built data models covering financial, inventory, sales, and production data. Recommended for Stage 2 and above organisations requiring self-service analytics.

Compatible: X3, Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+

Jet Reports

Excel-based reporting for Sage X3 — familiar interface for finance teams, live ERP data connection without manual exports. Useful for Stage 1 organisations building a reporting foundation.

Compatible: X3  ·  Stage: 1+

Qlik

Enterprise BI and data discovery platform. Recommended for larger organisations requiring cross-system analytics — ERP, CRM, and operational data in a unified model.

Compatible: X3, Intacct  ·  Stage: 3+
Expense Management

Webexpenses

Mobile-first expense management with automated policy enforcement and direct ERP posting to Sage Intacct and X3. Eliminates manual expense claim processing and provides real-time spend visibility.

Compatible: X3, Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+

Concur

SAP Concur for organisations requiring travel, expense, and invoice management at enterprise scale. Recommended for multi-entity businesses with significant travel budgets and complex approval hierarchies.

Compatible: Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+
Payables & Receivables Automation

Yooz

AI-powered AP automation — invoice capture, coding, approval routing, and ERP posting. Deep Sage Intacct integration. Recommended for organisations processing 200+ invoices per month.

Compatible: Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+

Paya

Integrated payments within Sage Intacct — ACH, credit card, and virtual card payments directly from the ERP. Eliminates manual payment processing and accelerates cash collection.

Compatible: Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+

X3CloudDocs PIA

For Sage X3 organisations, X3CloudDocs Purchase Invoice Automation provides equivalent AP automation capability — natively integrated without middleware.

Compatible: X3  ·  Stage: 2+
Warehouse Management (WMS)

Snapfulfil

Cloud WMS with bidirectional Sage X3 integration — inventory sync, GRN processing, pick-and-pack workflows, and despatch confirmation feeding directly into X3 stock and financial ledger.

Compatible: X3  ·  Stage: 2+
Manufacturing Execution (MES)

Infor

Production execution integrated with Sage X3 manufacturing module — shop floor data collection, labour and machine time recording, and quality management feeding X3 production costing in real time.

Compatible: X3  ·  Stage: 2+
Payroll & HR

Cintra

UK payroll and HR with direct integration to Sage Intacct — payroll journal posting, headcount data, and cost centre allocation. Recommended for UK organisations with complex payroll requirements.

Compatible: Intacct  ·  Stage: 1+

ADP

International payroll for organisations with multi-jurisdiction headcount. Sage Intacct integration for payroll cost posting and statutory reporting across multiple territories.

Compatible: Intacct  ·  Stage: 2+
Shipping & Logistics

Carrier integrations

Automated despatch confirmation, track-and-trace updates, and shipping cost allocation back to X3 sales orders — integrated with major UK and international carriers via the X3 EDI framework.

Compatible: X3  ·  Stage: 2+
