Governance Layer 2

The Tiered Approval Model

Every automated action has a defined human authority. The Tiered Approval Model ensures no automated process operates without accountability — from fully automated to CFO sign-off.

Tier Type Description
1
Fully automated
Routine, high-confidence transactions processed automatically with no human intervention required. Complete audit trail maintained.
2
Reviewer notification
Automated with notification to designated reviewer. 48-hour SLA for review. Reviewer can accept, query, or escalate.
3
Direct human review
AI-assisted processing but human review and approval required before posting. Used for exception scenarios and higher-value transactions.
4
CFO / FD sign-off
Highest-value or highest-risk transactions require direct CFO or Finance Director sign-off. Fully audited and timestamped.

Source: Mysoft Agentic Finance AI Governance Framework, Layer 2.
